# low-key-cinematic
Fingerprint: restrained, moody portrait drama with a believable narrative beat.
Palette and materials: black, umber, desaturated navy; wool, smoke, textured plaster, dark wood.
Camera: 85mm close or medium portrait with shallow depth. Light: single motivated side source and controlled falloff.
Photographed moment: listening at a doorway, considering a message, or turning into shadow; never neon pastiche or beauty-salon polish.
Exclusions: do not use high-key lifestyle brightness, vacation sunlight, e-commerce clarity, energetic sport motion, or fantasy effects.
