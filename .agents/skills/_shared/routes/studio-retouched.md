# studio-retouched
Fingerprint: controlled premium portrait, polished but still recognisably photographic.
Palette and materials: neutral grey, cream, muted jewel tones; paper sweep, satin, matte fabric.
Camera: 85mm head-and-shoulders or three-quarter portrait. Light: large soft key, subtle rim, clean catchlights.
Photographed moment: a deliberate pause between frames with natural breath and posture; never plastic skin or catalog-only neutrality.
Art-direction seeds: contrast formal control with one human asymmetry; use edge, fold, or interrupted symmetry as motif. Keep one dominant light decision and preserve material-specific highlights instead of polishing every surface equally.
Exclusions: do not use travel context, street documentary chaos, retro neon, fantasy scenery, or apparel-fit documentation.
