# ultra-close-real-face
Fingerprint: intimate, high-fidelity facial portrait that celebrates real human detail.
Palette and materials: natural skin tones, muted neutral background; visible pores, fine hair, minimal makeup texture.
Camera: 85–105mm tight face framing at eye level. Light: large diffused window or beauty dish with gentle falloff.
Photographed moment: a small inhale, blink, or half-smile; preserve asymmetry and unguarded expression.
Art-direction seeds: contrast facial control with one involuntary micro-event; use breath, moisture, fine hair, or asymmetric catchlight as motif. First read is expression, second read is real skin behavior, afterimage is withheld emotion. Do not add invisible wardrobe or lower-body detail.
Exclusions: do not use face-swapping or real-person likeness, plastic retouching, body-focused framing, fantasy effects, wide travel context, or apparel catalog coverage.
