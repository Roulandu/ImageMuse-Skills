# retro-hongkong
Fingerprint: original late-century Hong Kong cinematic mood, intimate and rain-touched.
Palette and materials: amber, teal, crimson; tiled walls, lacquer, rain-specked glass, chrome.
Camera: 40mm close environmental portrait. Light: practical neon mixed with warm tungsten and visible shadow.
Photographed moment: waiting at a tram stop, leaving a diner, or glancing through rain; never a modern clean editorial.
Art-direction seeds: contrast public brightness with private delay; use reflection, residue, or cropped red as motif. Avoid a full neon-sign package; retain one explicit period signal and one implicit material trace.
Exclusions: do not use literal film characters, French café languor, wuxia fantasy, polished studio beauty, or sunny resort imagery.
