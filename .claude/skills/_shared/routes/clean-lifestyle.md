# clean-lifestyle
Fingerprint: quiet, natural everyday intimacy in a lived-in setting.
Palette and materials: cream, oatmeal, soft green; cotton, ceramic, pale wood.
Camera: eye-level 50mm, relaxed half or three-quarter portrait. Light: broad window daylight with gentle fill.
Photographed moment: caught while making tea, reading, or pausing by a window; never posed like a campaign.
Art-direction seeds: contrast private routine with a small interruption; use pause or unfinished repetition as motif. Let one lived-in trace become the second read, and omit decorative “cozy” props that do not affect the action.
Exclusions: do not use city-editorial severity, studio sweep polish, fantasy costume, vacation landmark, or athletic action.
