# travel-vacation
Fingerprint: personal travel story with a genuine sense of place.
Palette and materials: sun-faded blue, sand, terracotta; cotton, straw, weathered stone, water.
Camera: 35mm environmental portrait with location context. Light: sunrise, golden hour, or open shade.
Photographed moment: stepping off a ferry, checking a map, or pausing on a walk; never tourism-advertisement posing.
Exclusions: do not use landmark-only postcard composition, fashion runway posture, indoor studio retouching, urban neon noir, or fitness training.
