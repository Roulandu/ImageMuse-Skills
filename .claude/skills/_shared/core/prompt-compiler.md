# Prompt Compiler

Keep the planning brief complete, then compile a selective final prompt. Do not make every field equally visible.

## Compilation order

Write the final Chinese prompt in this semantic order:

1. visual thesis and decisive moment;
2. subject-environment relationship and physical interaction;
3. composition, camera coordinates, projection, focal length, occlusion, and focus;
4. motivated light, exposure separation, and shadow consistency;
5. color roles and material dialogue;
6. visible face, hair, makeup, body, garment, lower-body, footwear, and accessory detail;
7. canvas and delivery requirements.

The canvas block may remain first when an existing output contract requires it, but treat it as a compact delivery prefix; begin the visual content immediately with thesis and moment.

## Selection rules

- Include all locked values, but merge them into the visual logic instead of repeating field labels.
- Include supplemental defaults only when they affect visible output.
- Preserve a full structured brief when the specialist contract requires it.
- In the final prompt, omit invisible details, duplicate adjectives, explanatory rationale, route names, and unused variations.
- Use one main style. A secondary influence may appear only as a restrained counterpoint and must not exceed the main style in visual weight.
- Keep negative constraints relevant to the actual failure risks.

## Causal check

Before returning, verify:

- camera choice follows the required viewer relation and space;
- pose follows the narrative beat and has support;
- light follows a source and reveals or hides something purposeful;
- color and material serve the thesis or hierarchy;
- environment changes the subject or action;
- removing generic quality adjectives does not erase the image identity.
