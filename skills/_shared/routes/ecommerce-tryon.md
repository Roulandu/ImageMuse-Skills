# ecommerce-tryon
Fingerprint: garment-first apparel presentation with accurate fit and construction.
Palette and materials: neutral background matching the garment; accurate fabric weave, seams, drape, and hardware.
Camera: 70mm full or three-quarter body, level and proportionally neutral. Light: even softbox illumination with truthful colour.
Photographed moment: a simple natural shift of weight or sleeve adjustment; never editorial distortion or seductive body emphasis.
Exclusions: do not use fashion-story city drama, studio glamour retouching, travel scenery, fantasy costume narrative, or sport action.
