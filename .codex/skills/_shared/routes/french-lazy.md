# french-lazy
Fingerprint: unhurried French-inspired elegance, intimate and slightly undone.
Palette and materials: ivory, faded red, tobacco; linen, knit, worn brass.
Camera: 50mm waist-up or seated portrait. Light: soft side-window light with restrained shadow.
Photographed moment: lingering over coffee, opening shutters, or looking away mid-thought; never high-fashion city urgency.
Exclusions: do not use clean Scandinavian lifestyle, Chinese heritage costume, glossy studio retouching, travel postcard framing, or gym action.
