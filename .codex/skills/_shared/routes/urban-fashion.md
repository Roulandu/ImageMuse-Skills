# urban-fashion
Fingerprint: contemporary city editorial with decisive graphic energy.
Palette and materials: charcoal, concrete, cobalt accents; denim, leather, steel, glass.
Camera: 35mm street-level three-quarter frame. Light: directional late-afternoon sun or controlled storefront spill.
Photographed moment: crossing a street or adjusting a jacket between destinations; never a product turntable.
Art-direction seeds: contrast graphic city order with one disrupted personal gesture; use crossing line, reflection, or a single accent color as motif. Define scene and camera axes so architecture directs attention without producing tangencies.
Exclusions: do not use pastoral domestic warmth, French softness, retro film nostalgia, studio beauty polish, or sports venue cues.
