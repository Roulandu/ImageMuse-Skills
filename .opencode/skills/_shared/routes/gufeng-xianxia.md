# gufeng-xianxia
Fingerprint: original guofeng/xianxia-inspired fantasy with tactile, cinematic worldbuilding.
Palette and materials: moonlit blue, mist grey, muted gold; layered silk, weathered stone, translucent veils.
Camera: 50mm medium portrait with atmospheric depth. Light: diffused moonlight or dawn haze, never harsh flash.
Photographed moment: pausing on a mountain path or turning in wind; preserve believable physical motion.
Art-direction seeds: contrast bodily weight with an environment that feels almost immaterial; use veil, mist boundary, or displaced wind as one motif. Use at most two cultural signals and one effects family; make fabric, hair, and particles share a force direction.
Exclusions: do not use modern Chinese minimalism, real historical person likeness, retro urban neon, studio seamless backdrops, or athletic competition.
