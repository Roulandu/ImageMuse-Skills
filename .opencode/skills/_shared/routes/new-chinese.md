# new-chinese
Fingerprint: modern Chinese aesthetics with contemporary restraint.
Palette and materials: jade, ink, warm white; silk, lacquer, bamboo, stone.
Camera: 50mm balanced portrait with architectural negative space. Light: soft daylight shaped by screens or eaves.
Photographed moment: moving through a tea room, gallery, or modern courtyard; never historical fantasy role-play.
Exclusions: do not use xianxia magic, retro Hong Kong neon, European café styling, commercial try-on framing, or stadium action.
